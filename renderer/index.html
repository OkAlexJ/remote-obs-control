Connect to OBS

Type the address of the PC running OBS at the top, then press Connect.

Where do I find that?
  1. In OBS on that PC open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings and tick Enable WebSocket server.
  2. Click Show Connect Info — it shows the IP, port (usually 4455) and password.
  3. Not on the same network? A Tailscale 100.x.x.x address works from anywhere.
